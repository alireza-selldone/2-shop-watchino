Open questions — need your decision

Eight open, one closed.

Question 9 was closed by the headless capture run. The other eight still need you.

  1. Composite swatches never actually render. You asked for the finish filter and for composites as a 135° gradient. The filter excludes composites, so all 14 are hidden and the gradient renderer is only exercised in code. Hide them entirely, or give them a separate “two-tone” row?
  2. Your discount count of 11 doesn’t match anything I can measure. Raw discount > 0 is 34; date-validated is 30; final_price < price is 30. If the dashboard says 11, the storefront and dashboard disagree and it’s worth finding out why.
  3. 30 of 35 still carry a reduction. Cards now hide the strikethrough entirely per your spec, so the grid reads clean — but the product page shows it on 30 references. Acceptable, or suppress above a price threshold?
  4. I changed a design token for contrast. --mist #8A9199 as body text is 2.69:1 on --dial, well under AA. I added --mist-text #616870 (4.75:1) used for text only; --mist still drives tick marks, rules and text on dark. Approve or revert.
  5. The struck-through price got darker. --whisper on white is 1.69:1. It now uses --mist-text. Still clearly muted against the graphite current price, but less faint than the prototype.
  6. Product descriptions are collection-level. Selldone holds no per-reference description, and article_pack.article.body for these products is mismatched demo copy — the watch 709403 returns text about a “SlimEdge Portable Monitor 15.6-Inch”. Each page says so plainly rather than inventing one.
  7. The bag is client-side only. Nothing is written to the shop and no order is created. The real physical-basket endpoints are documented inline at the top of checkout.js; wiring them would mutate the live shop, which I didn’t think was mine to do unasked.
  8. Two invented claims removed from approved copy. The hero said “238 components, 43 jewels” and the spotlight said “Two were made this year” — neither is in Selldone, and stock for that reference is 40. Both rewritten from real spec data. The rest of the marketing prose is untouched.
  9. CLOSED — scroll-linked behaviour is now verified with real frames. The pane still never composited, so captures were taken with headless Chromium instead. All four behaviours confirmed with before/after pairs in screens/: header 125px → 63px at scrollY 599 (announcement bar 38 → 0); rail marker 0 → 370px of a 677px track (54.7%); reveal 0 → 3 of 12 sections, first opacity 0 → 1; mobile buy bar translateY(99px) → none once the gallery leaves the viewport.
